Privacy Policy for Dragonhold

App: Dragonhold (com.carla.dragonhold) · Effective date: 25 June 2026

Short version: Dragonhold does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. Dragonhold has no user accounts, no login, and no registration. We do not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal or identifying information of any kind — including your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage data.

Game progress and saved data

Your game progress (your hold, dragons, and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see or access it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your saved progress is removed from your device.

Internet and network use

Dragonhold is a fully offline game. It does not connect to any server, send any data over the internet, or make any network requests during play.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind. There are no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software development kits (SDKs) in the app.

Third parties

We do not share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

Dragonhold is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no data at all, it does not gather personal information from children.

Permissions

Dragonhold does not request access to sensitive device features such as your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
